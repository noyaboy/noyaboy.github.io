I am an M.Eng. student at NYCU, conducting research in the Parallel Computing System Laboratory (PCS Lab) under Prof. Bo-Cheng Lai, where I also serve as IT manager. My thesis builds on traccc, the GPU track-reconstruction demonstrator of the ACTS project, in collaboration with CERN and the University of Washington, where I am an affiliate graduate student of the A3D3 Institute. A design-space exploration across GPUs, the AMD Versal V80 and MIMD manycores showed that this workload needs a custom accelerator whose architecture and compiler are co-designed around irregular memory access; I am now taping out that accelerator on TSMC 28nm. I am also second and corresponding author of UniTrac, an FPGA accelerator for GNN-based track reconstruction, co-developed with UW and UIUC.

I graduated from NTHU Electrical Engineering with distinction, with a GPA of 4.1/4.3 and a major-course GPA in the top 5% of 116 students, and received NTHU's Academic Excellence Award three times. At NYCU I received the Elite New Graduate Scholarship, and our FPGA tracking work received the Excellent Poster Award at TJCAS 2025. In Fall 2023 I was a teaching assistant for NTHU's General Physics Laboratory. Outside the lab, I dance, and in junior high I won the boys’ individual title at the national Tantui championship in Chinese martial arts. One motto guides me: we only get to do a few things, so each deserves to be done excellently.
