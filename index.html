Hao-Chun Liang

M.Eng. student · Institute of Pioneer Semiconductor Innovation, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University

I work on the hardware side of machine learning in the Parallel Computing System Laboratory, advised by Prof. Bo-Cheng Lai. As an affiliate trainee of the A3D3 Institute, I collaborate with researchers at the University of Washington and UIUC on real-time machine learning for high-energy physics.

My current focus is accelerating particle track reconstruction for the High-Luminosity LHC — high-level synthesis (HLS) accelerator design for the traccc pipeline on the AMD Versal V80, and CUDA-level optimization of GPU Kalman-filter tracking. In the long term, I aim to pursue a Ph.D. on the codesign of ML architectures and compilers.

Research Interests

  • ML architecture & compiler codesign — jointly optimizing model architectures and compilation stacks for efficient inference
  • Domain-specific accelerators — HLS-based FPGA/ACAP design (AMD Versal) and GNN inference for real-time scientific applications
  • GPU performance engineering — CUDA kernel optimization and profiling with Nsight Systems/Compute
  • Efficient machine learning — low-precision arithmetic and lightweight architectures for constrained deployment
